1. Input the size of the crossword grid (between 5 and 20) in the box provided at the top.
  2. Click on "Generate" to create the puzzle.
  3. Use the provided clues to fill in the crossword grid. Click on a cell and type your answer.
  4. Click on "submit" to check your answers.
  5. Click the "Generate new" button to create a new crossword puzzle.
  6. If you want to start over or correct mistakes, use the "Clear All" button to reset the grid.
